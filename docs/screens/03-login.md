## 2. Action Reference Table

| Marker | UI Element | Action Type | What to do |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| <kbd>1</kbd> | **Email Address** | **FILL IN** | Type your registered email address. |
| <kbd>2</kbd> | **Password** | **FILL IN** | Type your account password. Characters are masked for privacy. |
| <kbd>3</kbd> | **Remember me** | **OPTIONAL** | Check this box if you are on a personal computer to avoid logging in again each time. |
| <kbd>4</kbd> | **"Sign in" Button** | **CLICK HERE** | Click this button to validate your credentials and open your dashboard. |
| <kbd>5</kbd> | **"Create an account"** | **CLICK HERE** | Click this link if you don't have an account yet to open the registration form. |

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Enter your credentials**:
   - Type your email in the **Email Address** field (**[1]**).
   - Type your password in the **Password** field (**[2]**).
2. **Session options**:
   - Check **Remember me** (**[3]**) if you are on a private computer.
3. **Sign In**:
   - Click the blue **Sign in** button (**[4]**) or press <kbd>Enter</kbd> on your keyboard.
