# User Guide: Welcome & First Launch

[← Back to Master Index](../README.md) | [Next: 02 - Account Registration →](02-register.md)

---

The **Welcome screen** is the first page displayed when you launch Cadran for the very first time. It provides direct access to choose your language, start your registration, or sign in if you already have an account.

---

## 1. Visual Overview & Action Callouts

![Welcome Landing Page](../images/screens/01-welcome-screen-annotated.png)

---

## 2. Action Reference Table

| Marker | UI Element | Action Type | What to do |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| <kbd>1</kbd> | **Language Selector** | **SELECT** | Click the dropdown in the top-right corner to choose between English (`EN`) and French (`FR`). |
| <kbd>2</kbd> | **"Create an account"** | **CLICK HERE** | Click this primary button to open the registration form and set up your initial account. |
| <kbd>3</kbd> | **"Sign in" Button** | **CLICK HERE** | Click this button if your account is already created to go directly to the sign-in screen. |

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Choose your language**:
   - In the top-right navigation bar (marker **[1]**), select **EN** for English or **FR** for French.
2. **Start your initial registration**:
   - Click the blue **Create an account** button (marker **[2]**).
   - Alternatively, you can click **Register** in the top navigation bar.
3. **If you already have an account**:
   - Click the **Sign in** button (marker **[3]**) to access your login screen.
