# User Guide: Database Unlock

[← Previous: 04 - Database Encryption](04-database-encryption.md) | [Back to Master Index](../README.md) | [Next: 06 - Dashboard (Batch 2) →](06-dashboard.md)

---

The **Database Unlock** screen appears when starting Cadran if database encryption is turned on and automatic device unlock was not selected. You must enter your master passphrase to open your workspace.

---

## 1. Visual Overview & Action Callouts

![Database Unlock Screen](../images/screens/05-database-unlock-screen-annotated.png)

---

## 2. Action Reference Table

| Marker | UI Element | Action Type | What to do |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| <kbd>1</kbd> | **Master Passphrase** | **FILL IN** | Type your master encryption passphrase into this field. |
| <kbd>2</kbd> | **Reveal / Hide Icon** | **CLICK HERE** | Click the eye icon to show or hide the characters you are typing. |
| <kbd>3</kbd> | **Remember on this device** | **OPTIONAL** | Check this box if you want this computer to remember your key and skip this unlock prompt on next launch. |
| <kbd>4</kbd> | **"Unlock database"** | **CLICK HERE** | Click this button to unlock Cadran and access your accounts. |

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Enter your key**:
   - Click in the **Master decryption passphrase** field (**[1]**) and type your master key.
2. **Double check your typing (Optional)**:
   - Click the eye icon (**[2]**) to make sure there are no typos.
3. **Choose future startup behavior**:
   - Check **Remember on this device** (**[3]**) to avoid having to unlock manually next time.
4. **Unlock**:
   - Click the blue **Unlock database** button (**[4]**) or press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
   - You will be immediately taken to your accounts overview.
