# User Guide: Account Registration

[← Previous: 01 - Welcome Screen](01-welcome.md) | [Back to Master Index](../README.md) | [Next: 03 - Sign In →](03-login.md)

---

The **Account Registration** screen sets up your personal user profile on Cadran. Once created, Cadran automatically initializes standard income and expense categories for your workspace.

---

## 2. Action Reference Table

| Marker | UI Element | Action Type | What to do |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| <kbd>1</kbd> | **Full Name** | **FILL IN** | Type your display name or full name (e.g. `Alice`). |
| <kbd>2</kbd> | **Email Address** | **FILL IN** | Type your email address used to sign in. |
| <kbd>3</kbd> | **Password** | **FILL IN** | Choose a secure password (minimum 8 characters). |
| <kbd>4</kbd> | **Confirm Password** | **FILL IN** | Re-type the exact same password to confirm. |
| <kbd>5</kbd> | **"Sign up" Button** | **CLICK HERE** | Click this button to create your account and access your workspace. |
| <kbd>6</kbd> | **"Sign in" Link** | **CLICK HERE** | Click this link if you already have an account to switch to the sign-in form. |

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Enter your details**:
   - Click in the **Full Name** field (**[1]**) and type your name.
   - Click in the **Email Address** field (**[2]**) and enter your email address.
2. **Choose your password**:
   - Type your password in the **Password** field (**[3]**).
   - Re-type it in the **Confirm Password** field (**[4]**).
3. **Validate**:
   - Click the blue **Sign up** button (**[5]**).
   - You will be automatically logged in and taken to your accounts overview.
