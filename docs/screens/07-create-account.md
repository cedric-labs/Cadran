# User Guide: Create a Bank Account

[← Back to Dashboard](06-dashboard.md) | [Next: 08 - Edit & Configure Account →](08-edit-account.md)

---

The **Create Account** screen allows you to add an account to your personal budget workspace. You can choose an existing banking institution or register a new one, select the account category, and define the starting balance.

---

---

## 2. Action Reference Table

| Marker | UI Element | Action Type | What to do |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| <kbd>1</kbd> | **Bank Institution** | **SELECT** | Choose a bank from your registered institutions (e.g., BoursoBank, BNP Paribas) or select **"Create a new bank..."** to register a new one. |
| <kbd>2</kbd> | **Account Name** | **FILL IN** | Enter a descriptive label for the account (e.g., `Main Checking Account`, `Livret A Savings`). |
| <kbd>3</kbd> | **Account Type** | **SELECT** | Choose the account classification (Checking, Savings, Investment, or Credit Card). This dictates how balances roll up into your dashboard. |
| <kbd>4</kbd> | **Initial Balance** | **FILL IN** | Enter the opening balance of the account prior to your first imported bank statement (e.g., `2845.50`). |
| <kbd>5</kbd> | **"Create account"** | **CLICK HERE** | Click to validate and create the account. You will be redirected to the dashboard. |

---

## 3. Step-by-Step Procedure

1. **Select or create the bank institution**:
   - Use the dropdown (<kbd>1</kbd>) to select your financial institution.
   - If your bank is not yet in the list, choose the creation option and provide its name and color theme.
2. **Name your account**:
   - In the **Account name** field (<kbd>2</kbd>), enter a clear name to easily distinguish it from other accounts.
3. **Select the account type**:
   - In the **Account type** dropdown (<kbd>3</kbd>), select the appropriate type.
4. **Set your baseline opening balance**:
   - In the **Initial balance** field (<kbd>4</kbd>), enter your current account balance before importing older statements.
5. **Save the account**:
   - Click the green **Create account** button (<kbd>5</kbd>) to finalize.
