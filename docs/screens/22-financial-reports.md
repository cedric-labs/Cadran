The **Financial Reports & Analytics** dashboard gives you visual insight into your cash flow, category breakdowns, spending trends, and an interactive Pivot Table (TCD) for multi-dimensional analysis.

## 2. Action Reference Table

| Marker | UI Element | Action Type | What to do |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| <kbd>1</kbd> | **Main Views Selector** | **CLICK TAB** | Switch between Visual Dashboards (widgets & charts) and the interactive Pivot Table (TCD). |
| <kbd>2</kbd> | **"Manage Dashboards" Menu** | **CLICK HERE** | Open the menu to create a new custom dashboard or reset layout to factory default presets. |
| <kbd>3</kbd> | **Expense Breakdown Chart** | **INTERACT / HOVER** | Doughnut chart showing expense distribution across categories. Hover over arcs to see totals. |
| <kbd>4</kbd> | **Top Expenses Widget** | **INSPECT LIST** | Ranked list of your highest spending categories for the selected period with percentage shares. |
| <kbd>5</kbd> | **"Add Widget" Button** | **CLICK HERE** | Add new visualization cards (gauges, KPI numbers, bar charts, trend lines, or top lists). |

---

## 3. Pivot Table (TCD) Multi-Dimensional Exploration

When switching to the **Pivot Table** tab (<kbd>1</kbd>):
- Cross-tabulate your data across accounts, categories, and calendar periods.
- Drill down into specific rows or cells to view the exact underlying bank transactions.
- Save your custom pivot views for quick future consultation.

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Review your spending distribution**: Examine the breakdown chart (<kbd>3</kbd>) and top categories (<kbd>4</kbd>).
2. **Add a custom visualization**: Click **Add Widget** (<kbd>5</kbd>) and choose a chart type (Doughnut, Bar, KPI, Gauges).
3. **Switch to Pivot Table**: Click the **Pivot Table** pill (<kbd>1</kbd>) to explore historical aggregations across multiple criteria.
4. **Create a specialized dashboard**: Open the menu (<kbd>2</kbd>) and select **New Dashboard** to separate personal and business expenses.
