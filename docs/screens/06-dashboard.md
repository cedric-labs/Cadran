# User Guide: Financial Dashboard & Net Worth Overview

[← Back to Master Index](../README.md) | [Next: 07 - Create a Bank Account →](07-create-account.md)

---

The **Financial Dashboard** is the command center of Cadran. It displays your aggregated net worth, asset distribution across liquid accounts, savings, and debts, and gives quick access to each bank account ledger.

---

## 3. Key Financial Indicators

The top cards calculate your balance in real time:

- **Net Worth**: Total balance across all accounts (liquid assets + investments - liabilities).
- **Liquid Assets**: Immediately accessible funds from checking accounts and cash wallets.
- **Savings & Investments**: Total value of passbook accounts, term deposits, and investment portfolios.
- **Debts & Liabilities**: Total outstanding balances on deferred credit cards, loans, or overdrafts.

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Navigation

1. **Review your financial summary**: Check the four KPI cards to track balance changes and net worth trends.
2. **Access an account ledger**: Click the **Transactions** button (<kbd>4</kbd>) on any card to inspect its transaction history.
3. **Import new transactions**: Click **Import Statement** (<kbd>2</kbd>) to load transactions exported from your banking portal.
4. **Add a new financial institution**: Click **New Account** (<kbd>1</kbd>) to connect a new account to your workspace.
