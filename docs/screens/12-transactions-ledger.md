# User Guide: Unified Transaction Ledger

[← Back to Master Index](../README.md) | [Next: 13 - Edit Transaction & Category Details →](13-edit-transaction.md)

---

The **Unified Transaction Ledger** gives you a consolidated view of all debits, credits, and transfers across your accounts. From here, you can search, filter, reconcile, and categorize operations.

## 2. Action Reference Table

| Marker | UI Element | Action Type | What to do |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| <kbd>1</kbd> | **Search Filter Input** | **TYPE HERE** | Type keywords to filter transactions instantly by label, merchant name, or personal notes. |
| <kbd>2</kbd> | **Period Preset Selector** | **SELECT OPTION** | Choose a time range (Current Month, Previous Month, Last 90 Days, Current Year, or All Time). |
| <kbd>3</kbd> | **Advanced Filters Toggle** | **CLICK HERE** | Expand the multi-criteria filtering panel to isolate specific accounts, amount ranges, or categories. |
| <kbd>4</kbd> | **Category Assignment Badge** | **CLICK HERE** | Click directly on a transaction's category cell to open the category assignment and detail editor. |
| <kbd>5</kbd> | **Status Cycle Button** | **CLICK HERE** | Click on the status badge to cycle the operation between *Pending*, *Cleared*, and *Reconciled*. |
| <kbd>6</kbd> | **Split Receipt Badge** | **CLICK HERE** | Click on the split icon on multi-category transactions to open the split breakdown manager. |

---

## 3. Key Concepts & Statuses

Transactions move through three standard reconciliation states:

- **Pending**: The operation is noted or authorized but has not yet settled on your official bank statement.
- **Cleared**: The transaction appears on your bank statement and amounts match.
- **Reconciled**: The transaction is verified against an official monthly bank statement or closed accounting period.

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Instructions

1. **Locate an operation**: Type in the search box (<kbd>1</kbd>) or select a time period (<kbd>2</kbd>).
2. **Assign or update category**: Click the category badge (<kbd>4</kbd>) to adjust the categorization.
3. **Reconcile with statement**: Click the status indicator (<kbd>5</kbd>) once the charge is validated on your bank statement.
4. **Inspect multi-category breakdown**: If an expense spans multiple budgets (like supermarket groceries and home care), click the split badge (<kbd>6</kbd>) to view the individual parts.
